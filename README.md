# Evolution of CFD Methods (Chronological Classification)

This document categorizes **Conventional and Modern CFD Methods** based on their period of adoption/popularization.

---

## 1950s–1960s: Foundations of CFD
- **Finite Difference Method (FDM)** (1950s)
- **Finite Element Method (FEM)** (1960s)

---

## 1970s: General-Purpose Solvers
- **Finite Volume Method (FVM)**
- **Boundary Element Method (BEM)**
- **Panel Methods**
- **Potential Flow Methods**

---

## 1980s: Turbulence Modeling
- **Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS)**

---

## 1990s: High-Fidelity Simulations
- **Large Eddy Simulation (LES)**
- **Direct Numerical Simulation (DNS)**
- **Spectral Methods**

---

## 2000s: Alternative & Meshless Methods
- **Immersed Boundary Method (IBM)**
- **Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM)**
- **Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH)**
- **Meshless Methods (e.g., Finite Pointset, Moving Particle Semi-Implicit Method)**

---

## 2010s: Data-Driven & Accelerated Methods
- **Machine Learning-Assisted CFD**
- **Reduced-Order Surrogate Models**
- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)**
- **Uncertainty Quantification & Bayesian Methods**
- **Automated Mesh Refinement (AMR)**
- **GPU-Accelerated Solvers**

---

## 2020s: Intelligent & Integrated CFD
- **Digital Twin Integration**
  - Real-time CFD in Digital Twins
  - IoT & Sensor-Coupled Models
  - Predictive Maintenance & Control
- **Cloud-Native CFD Platforms** (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- **Exascale CFD (HPC + AI)**
- **Quantum CFD (Exploratory)**

---

📌 This chronological categorization highlights how CFD evolved from **numerical discretization methods** in the 1950s to **AI-driven and cloud-native intelligent platforms** in the 2020s.
